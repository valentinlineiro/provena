# Specimen #2: Agentic AI Engineer — Banking / Data Infrastructure Modernization

Project info:
Ready to shape the future of AI in finance? We’re looking for a talented Senior Consultant - Agentic AI Engineer to join one of the world's most renowned banking brands. Serving over 100 million customers across diverse businesses, you'll be a key player on a dynamic, remote team spanning the UK and Spain.

This project governs the large-scale legacy migration and modernization of data infrastructure. The primary objective is to convert their legacy Ab Initio ETL (Extract, Transform, Load) platform to a modern, cloud-native AWS Glue and PySpark environment. To accelerate this migration and optimize code generation, the team will leverage advanced generative AI patterns, specifically incorporating Claude Code and agentic/multi-agent workflows to automate and validate pipeline conversions.

Responsibilities:
Lead LLM Integration: Architect and implement advanced Generative AI solutions, serving as the primary expert for seamless Claude API integration and LLM orchestration.
Build Agentic Workflows: Design and deploy sophisticated agentic AI patterns, enabling autonomous, multi-step AI reasoning and execution within enterprise applications.
End-to-End AI Delivery: Drive the full lifecycle development of complex AI/ML systems, building on a track record of successfully bringing production-grade AI solutions to market.

Software Engineering & Architecture
Production-Grade Python Coding: Write, test, and optimize highly performant, production-ready code using expert-level Python, applying advanced design patterns and rigorous testing methodologies.
Distributed Systems & Big Data: Architect and maintain distributed systems and microservices, executing large-scale data processing pipelines (managing 1,000+ jobs) utilizing PySpark and distributed computing frameworks.
System Resilience: Implement robust error handling, comprehensive monitoring, and resilience protocols to ensure the continuous, reliable operation of high-availability AI systems.
Complex Debugging: Act as the ultimate escalation point for complex technical challenges, applying deep debugging expertise to resolve system issues efficiently.

Leadership & Team Collaboration
Technical Leadership: Lead, direct, and mentor AI/ML engineering teams, setting quality standards and fostering a culture of technical excellence.
Project Delivery: Oversee technical project execution, ensuring milestones are met on time and within strict quality standards, even under pressure.
Documentation & Communication: Produce clear, comprehensive technical documentation and communicate complex architectural decisions to both technical teams and wider stakeholders.
Cross-Functional Collaboration: Operate as a highly collaborative team player, aligning engineering efforts with broader business and product goals.
